Good questions

Everything you're wondering.

Does the person scanning need an app?

No. Any phone camera or NFC tap opens your emergency profile in a normal web browser. No app and no account are needed to help you — that's the whole point.

Is my personal data safe and private?

Yes. Nothing is public or searchable. Your profile only appears when someone scans your physical tag, and you decide exactly what information is shown. You can update or hide it whenever you like.

What if there's no internet where it's scanned?

The tag opens a web page, so a basic internet connection is needed to load your profile — the same connection used to make a call or open a map. In most public places in India that's readily available.

What happens if I lose my tag?

Your information lives in your account, not the tag itself. Deactivate the lost tag from your dashboard and activate a new one — your details carry over.

Does it work on all phones?

Yes. The QR code works on any smartphone camera, and NFC works on most modern phones with a simple tap.

How do I set it up?

Scan your new tag, create your profile in a couple of minutes, and activate. A printed guide comes with every tag. That's it — you're protected.
